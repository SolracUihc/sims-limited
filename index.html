About Us

Complete one-stop service

Our company was established in 2007 in Hong Kong. We specialize in providing a complete one-stop service for our customers—covering every stage from procurement and sample making to production and logistics.

In addition, we focus on genuine leather handbags, small leather goods, luxury accessories, and premium accessories. Whether you are looking to develop a new product or source high-quality items, our team works closely with you to ensure consistent quality, efficient production, and reliable delivery.

With a streamlined workflow and dedicated support, we help clients turn ideas into samples, scale up manufacturing smoothly, and ship products with confidence worldwide.
